SDoH Predictive Visualization Study

Welcome

Thank you for taking the time to review these visualization tools. This web application is designed to support a focus group study about how practitioners understand and use predictive model explanations in healthcare and social service settings.

The system focuses on predictions related to emergency department utilization and social determinants of health, such as food access, housing stability, healthcare access, and social connectedness. The goal is not to replace professional judgment, but to explore whether different visualizations can help users better understand why a system makes a recommendation.

During the session, we will walk through three visualization approaches. After each visualization, we will ask questions about what you think the display is showing, how easy it is to understand, how useful it might be in practice, and what concerns or improvements you would suggest.

Study Flow

1. Decision Tree Visualization

Shows the step-by-step logic of one decision tree model and allows users to highlight specific SDoH features.

2. Sunburst Visualization

Shows patterns across multiple decision trees to help identify consistent drivers of risk across models.

3. Interactive Dashboard

Allows users to enter patient-related values and view a prediction, probability, and explanation pathway.
