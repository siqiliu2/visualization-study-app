Notes for practitioners

Use probabilities as decision support, not as the sole basis for action.
  • What you’re seeing: the model’s estimated probability of the positive class (risk=1) at the reached leaf.
  • How to interpret: higher p(risk=1) means the tree has seen similar profiles in training that were more often labeled high risk.
  • Transparency: the “Decision path” lists each split and your value at that split.
