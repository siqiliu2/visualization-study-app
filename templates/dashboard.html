Notes for practitioners

Demo probabilities illustrate this visualization; they are not fitted patient risks.
  • What you’re seeing: the same synthetic demonstration probabilities as the first visualization.
  • How to interpret: a probability ≥ 0.5 displays High ED Utilization; a probability < 0.5 displays Low ED Utilization. Sample counts are unavailable.
  • Transparency: the “Decision path” lists each visited split and your value. Omitted branches have no final prediction. Printed split thresholds may be rounded.
